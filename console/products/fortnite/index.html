Fortnite for PlayStation 4

Scooby Fortnite is in early development for PlayStation 4. The shared menu and settings foundation is prepared; native PS4 integration and gameplay testing are still ahead. Downloads, gameplay screenshots and a showcase will follow as the port progresses.

Menu and customization preparation

  • Aim, Visuals, Misc, Settings and Testing menu structure, with Unlocks under Testing
  • Shared themes, per-feature styles and controller keyboard components
  • English, German, Spanish, French and Russian language preferences
  • Shared crosshair and console rendering, plus configuration support; PS4 saving and controller integration are pending

Combat feedback preparation

Hit markers and damage numbers are implemented in the shared test environment. They still need verified Fortnite damage events and PS4 rendering before they can work in game.

Release status

No PS4 plugin has been released. Native aim, player visuals, radar, visibility checks, spectator detection, weapon controls and unlocks are unavailable while the game integration is being built.

Release date, firmware requirements and installation steps will be published after console validation. Follow Scooby Discord for updates, or browse Console products.
