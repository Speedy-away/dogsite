Scooby R6 1.0.2

A controller-operated GoldHEN plugin for Rainbow Six Siege, EU CUSA01788, game version 03.71. Organize your setup through Aim, Visuals, Misc, Settings and Testing, with saved profiles and an on-screen controller keyboard.

What is new in 1.0.2

  • Corrected visibility scene/interface validation and active-life target filtering, including targets that retain positive HP after becoming inactive.
  • Pixel-aligned PT Sans ESP text, including Cyrillic support; existing font choices migrate automatically.
  • Operator names and icons share one Operator section. The old Visual defaults sub-tab is removed.
  • The target-line preview and watermark can remain visible while the menu is open. Spectators and Console sit below Open hint in Overlays.
  • Bullet tracers have independent Local, Team and Enemy toggles, colors, width, lifetime, fade and outline controls. A bounded queue retains the newest shots during bursts.

Included controls

  • Lock On and Silent aim controls, separate X/Y smoothing, Hold/Toggle/Always activation, Enemy/Team/All targets and selectable hitbox groups.
  • Triggerbot with 0–50 ms delay, plus independent Recoil and Spread strength controls.
  • Enemy/Team visuals: boxes, fill, skeleton/head circles, health bars/text, player/operator/weapon names, distances, off-screen arrows, colors, outlines and offsets.
  • Gadget names/icons with Attack, Defense and Shared profiles and per-item toggles; configurable radar and crosshair.
  • Named profiles, auto save, confirmed deletion, themes, UI images, RGB/Hex entry and English, German, Spanish, French and Russian language options.

Install and compatibility

Open or close the menu with R1 + Square. The install ZIP includes the plugin, GoldHEN entry, setup guide, licenses, checksums and known issues. Fully close Siege and relaunch after replacing the plugin. This download targets CUSA01788 03.71.

Validation and current limits

Host regression tests and PS4 artifact checks accompany the package. Final console verification of aim, firing, visibility and weapon-control behavior remains in progress. Native Vischeck is connected behind callback/TLS guards; live spectator detection and Bomb-site classification remain incomplete. Some optional engine providers are unavailable. Testing / Unlock All is session-only and does not grant persistent inventory. See the included known issues for the full scope.
