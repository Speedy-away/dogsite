Features

Scooby Overwatch brings configurable on-screen information and FOV controls to a PS4 menu operated with R1 + Square. Enemy and team visuals can be adjusted and stored in profiles.

Visuals

  • Enemy / Team ESP — Separate visual settings with coral enemy and cyan teammate colors.
  • Boxes, health bars and snaplines — Configurable player visuals.
  • Distance and off-screen indicators — Metric distance labels and off-screen markers.
  • Radar — Matching enemy and teammate contacts.

Menu & FOV

  • FOV — Adjustment from 70–120°.
  • Controller menu — Open with R1 + Square.
  • Configuration profiles — Save your preferred visuals, layout and colors.
