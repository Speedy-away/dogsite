YOUR FRONTIER, YOUR SETUP

Scooby RDR2 v1.0.0, on PlayStation 4.

Scooby RDR2 brings a configurable list menu to your PS4, with organized pages for Self, Network, Weapons, Vehicle, Teleport, World, Spawner and Settings. Adjust your interface, explore local tools and keep frequently used options within reach of the controller.

Open or close the menu with R1 + Square. Use the D-pad to navigate, X to select, Circle to go back and Square to search while the menu is open. Text and numeric fields use controller text entry.

Self & appearance

Movement controls, walking Noclip, animations, appearance pages and saved outfits. Model changes and individual outfit presets remain in gameplay testing.

Weapons & visuals

Organized loadout, ammo and crosshair pages, weapon visual settings and separate ESP settings for players, NPCs, horses, vehicles and self.

Explore the frontier

Waypoint and objective teleports, saved locations, world controls and model-based spawner pages. Individual models and treasure behavior are still being tested.

Make it yours

Search, themes, colors, menu size and position, backgrounds, descriptions, overlays and saved configuration profiles.

PS4 status: menu navigation and ESP have been observed in Online gameplay. The screenshot shows a development build; individual features, full Story/Online coverage and console performance remain under validation. The PC feature catalogue describes a separate edition.

Prepare your PS4 account

The PS4 version creates /data/Scooby/account.json if it is missing. Enter your Scooby username and password using FTP, or use Download account.json below Redeem key in the account portal.

Account files are for console/PS4 and are not needed for PC. Keep your file private and restart the game after editing it. Premium access uses the existing Scooby RDR2 product. Already own an active RDR2 license? Sign in with that account; you do not need to buy it again.

Without verified Premium, free mode follows the shared PS4 access switch. Offline fallback grants free access only.

Looking for the PC edition?

The Windows edition is separate. Visit the Red Dead Online mod menu for PC for its features, screenshots and setup.
