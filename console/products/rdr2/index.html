What is in the beta?

Player, weapons & visuals

  • Self & appearance — Health, cores, movement, Walking Noclip, animations, models, wardrobe and saved outfits.
  • Weapons & aim — Categorized loadout, ammo, weapon effects, aim settings and crosshair controls. Recent aim, Explosion Gun, Entity Shooter and Bullet Swap changes await live acceptance.
  • ESP — Separate player, NPC, animal, vehicle and self settings, with colors, labels and distance controls.

Explore & manage

  • Teleport — Waypoint, objective, destination categories and saved locations.
  • World & spawners — Weather/time, entity management, bodyguard and pet managers, and spawn categories.
  • Network & recovery — Player pages, selected/all-player controls, cages, treasure and recovery requests. Effects depend on game state, entitlement and available providers.

Make it yours

  • Controller menu — R1 + Square to open, D-pad to navigate, X to select, Circle to go back and Square to search.
  • Appearance & profiles — Themes, colors, position, scale and saved settings.

v1.0.0 Beta · release notes

For EU CUSA08519, game version 01.32, using a GoldHEN-capable PS4. Other regions and versions are not established by this release.

  • Includes menu routing and Back-navigation improvements, merged World/Misc, updated weapon controls and recovery batch continuation after definitive rejection.
  • Recovery separates request/receipt status from actual character XP readback. An accepted request or successful receipt does not establish a payout; the individual 25k XP action remains unconfirmed.
  • This normal build retains account authentication and Premium entitlement checks. It does not force Premium access like the development screenshots.

Beta limitations: This exact public build has not been tested live on a console. Full Story/Online coverage, performance, remote effects, treasure lifetime, recent weapon/aim changes and respawn navigation remain under validation. Incoming protection hooks, player portraits and IP/geolocation are unavailable. Rewards and XP amounts are not guaranteed.
