PS4 v1.0.0 feature overview. Menu navigation and ESP have been observed in Online gameplay; this catalogue documents current controls, not verified support for every feature. Model changes, spawning, treasure lifetime, full Story/Online coverage and performance remain in testing. Visibility and synchronization of effects with other players remain under validation. Character/role XP awards, Fossils, Eggs, All Collections, portraits, IP/geolocation and protection hooks require providers that are not active in this build.

Current request controls

Money & Gold requests

Individual requests, loop toggles, batch size and local delay controls. Game eligibility and reward processing determine payouts; a locally accepted request does not confirm a reward.

Inventory collection sets

Collections live under Recovery → XP. Inventory-based sets have give and loop controls; Fossils, Eggs and All Collections require a Loot route that is not available in this PS4 build.

Recovery status & stop

Inspect local loop activity or stop pending recovery work. Character/role XP awards and protection hooks are not active providers in this PS4 build.
