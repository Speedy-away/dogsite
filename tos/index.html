Payments and payment disputes

A chargeback is a request to your bank or payment provider to reverse a payment.

  • We may hold, reject, or cancel payments marked as suspicious, fraudulent, or high risk.
  • If a payment is flagged, we will not issue a product key. The payment may be held or returned to the cardholder.
  • Our policy prohibits chargebacks: filing one leads to permanent loss of access and a block on future purchases. Threatening a chargeback also leads to an immediate ban. This policy does not remove rights you have under applicable law.
  • We keep purchase and transaction records and may use them to respond to fraudulent payment disputes.

Paying with cryptocurrency

Check the payment instructions carefully before sending cryptocurrency.

  • Check the address and amount. You are responsible for sending the amount requested to the correct wallet address.
  • Allow for network fees. If fees reduce the amount received below the amount requested, the payment will be incomplete.
  • Blockchain transfers cannot normally be reversed. Our policy is not to refund cryptocurrency purchases, except where required by applicable law.
  • We are not responsible for mistakes such as sending to the wrong address, sending the wrong amount, or failing to cover network fees.

Updates and maintenance

Buying a product while it is being updated does not give you immediate access.

  • Under our policy, update delays do not qualify for refunds, compensation, or extra access time, except where applicable law requires otherwise.
