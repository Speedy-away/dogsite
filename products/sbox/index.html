Included with your Scooby account

Scooby S&box is online and free for every authenticated free-key or paid account. Open S&box in the loader, locate the game if needed, and launch. The loader downloads and verifies the matching runtime files for you. No additional S&box key is required.

Combat

  • Lock-on and silent aim: switch aim modes from the Legit tab, with adjustable FOV and range. Lock-on includes smoothness controls.
  • Multi-hitbox selection: head, neck, chest, stomach, pelvis, arms and legs. Near selects the chosen hitbox closest to the crosshair.
  • Target controls: include players or NPCs, require visibility, ignore teammates and keep the current target.
  • Break aim: stop on manual mouse movement, with an adjustable threshold.
  • Triggerbot: fire when the crosshair hits a valid target, with a configurable delay and its own activation key.
  • Aim overlays: FOV circle, target hitbox dot and target line, with adjustable colors, opacity and size.
  • Activation: press-to-bind aim and trigger keys with Hold, Toggle or Always modes.

Players, NPCs and held weapons

  • Separate ESP styles: configure players, NPCs and your own model, with distance limits and an all-skinned-models fallback.
  • Player information: boxes, skeletons, names, distance labels, health bars and health gradients.
  • Aim-of-sight lines: show the direction a player is facing; adjust line length, width and color.
  • Weapon names: show the current held weapon, with text size and position controls.
  • Chams and snaplines: visible and through-wall highlights, outlines and configurable line origins.
  • Held visuals: separate arms and weapon chams with individual colors.

Items, entities and radar

  • Item and entity ESP: names, boxes, distances, chams and snaplines, with independent styling.
  • Filters and selection: match names or components, limit range and select individual entities from the entity list.
  • Draggable radar: player and NPC markers, teammate colors, optional names, rotation, rings, crosshair and outline.
  • Radar appearance: adjust size, range, marker size, shape, background and colors.

World visuals

  • Night mode: choose the tint and strength.
  • Sky controls: custom sky tint and brightness.
  • Fog controls: remove fog or apply a custom fog tint.

Movement and camera

  • Movement: bunny hop and auto strafe.
  • Camera: FOV override and third person with distance, shoulder and height offsets.
  • Viewmodel: weapon/model FOV adjustment and forward, sideways and vertical offsets, plus reset controls.
  • Crosshair: custom size and color.

Interface and profiles

  • Custom appearance: accent colors and color pickers with alpha controls.
  • UI scaling: a 25–200% slider, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, 125%, 150% and 200% presets, plus automatic resolution scaling.
  • Feature hotkeys: Hold, Toggle and Always modes, an assigned-key list and a clear-all button.
  • Search and favorites: find features quickly and keep frequently used options together.
  • Overlays: draggable watermark with an inline FPS graph and an assigned-hotkey display.
  • Saved configs: create, save and load named profiles; open the config directory directly.

Getting started

  1. Download the Scooby Loader and sign in with your existing free key or paid account.
  2. Close S&box, select its card in the loader, then choose Launch S&Box.
  3. Use Locate game if the loader cannot find your Steam install.
  4. Press F1 or Insert to open the menu, then set your preferred menu key and save a config.

Game-mode support

Available data and controls vary by game mode. ESP displays information exposed by the active scene. Silent aim currently supports first-person weapons using the standard combat-weapon firing path; custom weapons and third-person aiming may not support it.
