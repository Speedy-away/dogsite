MAKE IT YOURS

More control over every scene.

Scooby brings one organized cheat interface to the 32-bit Windows versions of Left 4 Dead 1 and Left 4 Dead 2. It is included with every authenticated free or paid account; there is no separate L4D purchase.

Use the loader's Start Game popup to choose either game. The dashboard detects running games and lets you choose explicitly when both are open.

Next release: the latest model previews, Capture Protection, asset downloads and display-mode improvements are prepared for the next loader update. Live game and OBS/Discord acceptance checks are still pending.

Infected & pickup ESP

Identify survivors, common and uncommon infected, witches, tanks and special infected. Customize filled or gradient boxes, skeletons, HP text and type colors. Place health, names and distance independently on any side. Give dropped weapons, medical supplies, ammo and items their own visuals; available types follow the selected game.

Native glow & team HUD

In L4D2, outline survivors, special infected, common infected and pickups with separate glow colors, flashing and distance controls. Keep teammate names and health visible in a movable HUD with an incapacitated color and adjustable width.

Rage & Legit aim

Choose Camera, Silent or pSilent with independent auto fire for each profile. Prioritize the crosshair, special infected, survivors or common infected. Tune hitboxes, range, FOV, lock-on and visibility checks, with camera smoothing for Legit. Shot aiming and auto fire respect reloads, ammunition and weapon cooldowns.

Triggerbot & Anti-Aim

Use separate Legit and Rage triggerbot settings with target filters, hitboxes, delay and activation keys. Configure recoil and spread controls, plus Anti-Aim pitch, yaw, inversion, jitter and spin.

Independent chams

Customize players, self, arms, held and dropped weapons, items and infected types. Visible, Invisible and Overlay have independent materials, colors and animation. Material and layer animation controls share one group, with eight animation modes and separate speed and strength settings.

Movement & exploits

Pair bunnyhop and auto strafe with Speed hack and extra movement ticks, or Lag exploit and its sequence increment. Use Airstuck to hold position, save a grounded destination and return with a separate Teleport key.

Game controls

Enable Anti fall damage, Anti AFK, No push and SvPure bypass for local sessions. Release Smoker pins and, in L4D2, Jockey rides, and remove the boomer screen overlay. Ping reducer lowers interpolation delay; it does not change network latency.

World, view & overlays

Tint world materials, props and sky. In Self → View, adjust camera and viewmodel FOV, weapon position and third-person distance or shoulder position. Keep radar icons, per-type radar colors, spectators, movement telemetry, hotkeys and an FPS watermark on screen.

Lua, configs & languages

Build custom pages, overlays and tools with the Lua editor and L4D host API. Mark script features as compatible with Capture Protection or automatically disabled while it is enabled. Save profiles, choose from 23 language catalogs, adjust text, UI and overlay scale, assign hotkeys and keep controls in Favorites.

Interactive 3D previews

Check ESP and chams on survivor, common infected, special infected, weapon and item models. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom and double-click to reset. The chams Target selector sits beside the preview, which cycles through random matching models while idle.

Capture Protection

Keep compatible menu and overlay drawing on a separate capture-excluded surface. Chams, native glow, world effects, camera changes and native Lua hooks are disabled. Blocked controls blur or dim and explain why on hover. Turning protection off unlocks them without automatically restoring cleared switches.

In-game asset downloads

Follow a progress bar showing the current file, bytes and percentage as required assets download. Verified cached files are reused; missing or damaged files are downloaded again. Press F5 to retry a failed download without losing completed files.

Resolution & display modes

The interface refits when resolution or DPI changes and retains its state through supported windowed/fullscreen transitions, resizing, minimizing and Alt-Tab. Capture Protection uses borderless fullscreen. A failed DXVK exclusive-mode reset falls back to borderless.

Local / Host controls

On a session you host, use Godmode, infinite reserve ammo and no reload, or inspect health, speed, position and the current weapon. Godmode and no reload affect all players on your hosted server and restore the previous server setting when disabled.

Weapons, items & zombie spawner

Browse the selected game's Weapons, Items, Ammo and Zombies. Spawn one to ten copies, adjust placement distance, choose a random clear location or spawn infected dead. These controls require local server authority.

One look for the whole campaign

Set a default appearance in Global, then give individual campaigns their own look with Override Global. Dead Center has one card for Hotel, Streets, Mall and Atrium. Your settings stay together as you move between chapters.

Both games, every campaign

Browse 6 L4D1 and 14 L4D2 official campaigns, including DLC. No Mercy, Dark Carnival, The Parish and the rest each have one entry. L4D1 co-op and versus chapters share the campaign look.

Maps & looks

Search campaign cards, browse built-in looks or saved presets, and preview before applying. Use Map Original as your starting point, Apply look, Undo look or Copy Global settings. Save a named look to use again.

Sky & Lighting

The dedicated Sky and Lighting sub-tabs stay available. Choose from 12 sky presets, adjust brightness and tint, animate sky colors or tune world breathing. Edit Global or the selected campaign override.

Clearer previews

Larger campaign cards preserve their artwork proportions. Sky looks use full sky panoramas, with separate world, prop and sky color swatches. Preview images show the artwork and textures; the final scene depends on the map.

Independent Materials

Materials keeps the built-in chams library and custom materials. Choose from 27 styles, with independent Visible, Invisible and Overlay layers, colors and animation. These controls remain separate from campaign looks.

Your materials & shortcuts

Open VFX → Materials → My materials, add matching name.vmt and name.vtf files, then press Refresh. Add a matching PNG or GIF for a preview. Existing hotkeys, Lua controls and saved profiles remain available.

The next release downloads its built-in skies, materials and model previews in game, with progress and retry controls; no manual image pack is needed. Supported sky textures can be replaced, while unrecognized custom-map sky formats keep their original appearance.

Common questions

Does Capture Protection work with OBS and Discord?

It requires Windows 10 2004 or newer and uses Windows capture exclusion for the separate menu and overlay surface. Results depend on the recording or sharing method; test your actual OBS output or Discord share. Live recorder acceptance is still pending for the next release. It does not hide gameplay actions or changes made by independent third-party scripts or modules.

Why are some controls blurred or disabled?

Capture Protection disables features that change the game image, including chams, native glow, world effects and camera changes. Hover over a blocked control for the explanation. Disable Capture Protection to unlock it, then enable the feature again yourself; your tuning and shortcuts are retained.

Can I switch between windowed and fullscreen?

The upcoming display update retains interface state through supported mode and resolution changes. Capture Protection converts fullscreen to borderless while retaining the render resolution. Disabling protection restores the game's fullscreen intent, with a borderless fallback if DXVK rejects exclusive mode. Live in-game acceptance is still pending.

What does the asset progress bar download?

The next release downloads required fonts, model previews and VFX assets when it loads, then opens the full interface after verification. Cached files are reused. If the download fails, press F5 to retry; completed files are retained. Your configs, custom materials and edited scripts keep their existing locations.

Do I need an L4D subscription?

No. L4D is included with any authenticated paid account or valid free-key session. Existing free keys retain their normal expiration.

Does it support both games?

Yes, through separate verified adapters in one 32-bit module. Choose the desired game in the loader; it automatically selects a newly detected single running game. Native glow and Anti Jockey are L4D2-only.

Why are L4D features inactive after loading?

Start a local single-player map. Gameplay features are suspended on remote servers, during demo playback, in loading screens and when player data is unavailable. Check the feature toggle and hotkey mode; the L4D troubleshooting guide covers the remaining checks.

How do I choose L4D1 or L4D2 and open the interface?

Select the game in the loader's Start Game popup, wait for its main menu, then press Load. If both games are running, choose the target in the dashboard dropdown. Open the interface with Insert after loading. See L4D setup and Windows and game requirements.

Which renderers are available?

The Windows game integration uses Direct3D 9. Vulkan/DXVK can be used when the installed game provides that renderer. The OpenGL UI backend and preview are available for verified compatible ports; stock Windows L4D installations do not provide a native OpenGL game renderer.

Do campaign looks apply to every chapter?

Yes. One Dead Center override applies to Hotel, Streets, Mall and Atrium. The same grouping covers all 20 official campaigns across L4D1 and L4D2. Campaigns without an enabled override use Global. Additional custom map IDs can still be added separately.

Can I still use the VFX sub-tabs?

Yes. Maps & looks, Sky, Lighting and Materials remain available in both games. Sky and Lighting edit Global or your selected campaign override; Materials keeps its independent chams and custom-material controls. Hotkeys and Lua feature controls are preserved.

Can I use my own materials and previews?

Yes. Use a supported VertexLitGeneric or UnlitGeneric VMT with a same-name VTF. Put a same-name PNG or GIF beside it for an automatic preview, then press Refresh. GIF is preview artwork; the game still uses the VTF. The supplied example shows the supported TextureScroll setup.

Can I write custom Lua features?

Yes. The API reference covers snapshots, infected and pickup queries, projected boxes, bones, rendering, hotkeys and custom controls. Lua reads copied game data; scripts do not run as the game's VScript engine.
