Scooby for Half-Life 1 is a customizable menu for the original Steam game on Windows, using its 32-bit GoldSrc OpenGL renderer. Adjust entity visuals, model materials, radar, hotkeys and interface settings, with local Lua pages and saved profiles.

Supported games: Half-Life 1, Half-Life: Blue Shift, Half-Life: Opposing Force and Half-Life: Source. All four share this product page, feature library and setup guide. Select your installed edition in the Scooby loader for its matching files and settings.

ESP & radar

Boxes, names, distances, skeletons and snaplines for supported entities, with category filters and a configurable radar.

Animated chams

Tinted, Flat, Wireframe and Additive styles, visible and occluded colors, plus Pulse, Color fade and Rainbow animation.

Lua & profiles

Build local menu pages, read supported session and entity information, and keep settings in separate game profiles.

Campaign controls

Godmode, noclip, ammo controls, movement and spawners are verified for Half-Life and Opposing Force on Windows. Their availability follows the selected edition and build.
