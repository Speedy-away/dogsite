Description

Customize combat with lock on, sniper silent aim, Magic bullet, triggerbot, recoil control and separate target snaplines. Configure ESP for players, NPCs, self, vehicles and pickups, with health bars, skeletons, radar and the Niko preview.

Find vehicles and weapons by name or model, filter by class and episode, and configure spawning, ammunition and equipment. The menu also includes health and wanted controls, movement, camera settings, vehicle repair and handling, saved-location teleports, time and weather, configs, hotkeys and Lua.

Latest local DLL updates: Quick spawn, scrolling catalog lists, an attached vehicle information panel, separate NPC and Niko previews, weapon ammunition labels, smooth health bars with HP text in Health settings, and 3D vehicle and pickup boxes. These updates are built locally and are not yet confirmed in the published loader release. Pickup names and fire/explosive ammo remain unfinished.

Supported builds: exact GTA IV 1.0.7.0, 1.0.8.0 and Complete Edition 1.2.0.59 Windows x86 executables. Standalone EFLC, other executable builds and projectile/explosive silent aim are not supported.

Sign in with premium access or a shared free key, start GTA IV and load a save, then choose GTA IV in Scooby Loader. The loader downloads and verifies the DLL and confirms menu startup. First use downloads fonts and languages; later starts reuse the cache. Press F1 or Insert to open the menu.

Restart GTA IV before loading a replacement. Configs, scripts and UI assets stay in C:/scooby/GTA4. Release: 2026.10.10.7.
