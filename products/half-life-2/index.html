FREE ACCESS

Return to City 17.

Scooby for Half-Life 2 is released. Select your game in the loader to get started.

Campaign edition

This release covers the original Half-Life 2 campaign. Episodes are not included.

Windows support

Windows x86 with Direct3D 9. Linux and Proton are unverified.

Choose your game

Looking for multiplayer? Deathmatch has its own product page and game version in the loader. Explore Half-Life 2: Deathmatch.

Compatibility

Compatibility: combat, chams, restoration and performance have not been fully verified in every supported game and architecture.

GET STARTED

Load Half-Life 2.

  1. Open Scooby and go to Source Games.
  2. Select Half-Life 2 & Deathmatch, then choose Half-Life 2 under Game version.
  3. Enable Start game to launch the selected game, or open Half-Life 2 yourself.
  4. Press Load and follow the loader's status.
