MEGABONK FEATURES

More control over every run

Explore the complete feature list →

Scooby for Megabonk brings player controls, build modifiers and progression tools together in one customizable menu. Fine-tune your health, damage and XP, try different movement and attack settings, or add pickups and items to experiment with your build.

Health and abilities

Use God mode, replenish health and shield, choose your HP limits, and control active ability and Flex passive readiness.

Damage, XP and upgrades

Adjust attack damage, multiply earned XP and upgrade bonuses, lock your level, and set rerolls, banishes and skips.

Movement and rewards

Tune movement speed, jump force, extra jumps, attack speed and projectile speed. Adjust gold gain or hold your chosen gold amount.

Pickups and items

Choose from 11 pickup types, set quantities and spawn distance, search for items to add to your inventory, or collect all XP in your run.

Progression and unlocks

Manage silver and unlock individual characters, weapons, tomes, items, maps or hats. Unlock a whole category when you want more choices. Unlock actions save your progression.

Your menu setup

Choose V1 or V2, save profiles, assign hotkeys and favorites, and select from 23 languages. Download language updates while preserving your custom translations.

Lua and custom HUDs

Use Scripts, Lua Editor and Console with the Megabonk game API. Read player and run stats, control features, queue actions and start from four bundled examples.

Guides for every step

Find setup, languages, configs, hotkeys, Lua and spawner help in the Megabonk wiki.
