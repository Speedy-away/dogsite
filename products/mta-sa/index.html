Description

Scooby MTA brings targeting, visuals and world tools into one customizable menu for Multi Theft Auto: San Andreas. Set up separate ESP profiles for players, NPCs, vehicles and yourself, adjust aim controls, and keep track of streamed players on the world map.

Make the interface your own with hotkeys, saved profiles, themes and scaling. Use the Lua editor to build personal overlays and tools, or start with the bundled examples.

  • Aim controlsTarget filters, smoothing, prediction and configurable activation.
  • ESP & radarIndependent profiles, health bars, skeletons and appearance controls.
  • World toolsSan Andreas map, player markers and on-foot teleport controls.
  • Lua & customizationScripts, editor, profiles, themes and flexible hotkeys.
