Product Description

Overview

Scooby RDR2 is a premium Red Dead Redemption 2 Online mod menu with 739 features spread across 10 tabs. From self protection and weapons to world control, recovery and a full Lua scripting engine, it is the most complete RDR2 Online toolkit available.

It ships with two interfaces you can run side by side - a pointer-driven ClickUI panel with tabs for Main, Outfits, Model Changer, Animations and Particles, and a keyboard-driven ListUI for players who prefer a classic list menu. Switch between them at any time, or keep both on screen.

Browse the complete list on the All RDR2 Features page, which is generated directly from the menu source.

Self & Player Options

  • Protection - God mode, no ragdoll, anti lasso, anti hogtie, anti melee, and anti AFK
  • Visibility - Invisible, off the radar, and never wanted
  • Health & Stamina - Keep all bars and cores filled, auto heal, infinite stamina, unlimited oxygen, fireproof, explosion proof
  • Movement - Super run, super jump, walk on water, swim fast, superman flight, noclip, and free cam
