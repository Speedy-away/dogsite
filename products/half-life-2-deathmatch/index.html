GET STARTED

Load Half-Life 2: Deathmatch.

  1. Open Scooby and go to Source Games.
  2. Select Half-Life 2 & Deathmatch, then choose Half-Life 2: Deathmatch under Game version.
  3. Enable Start game to launch Deathmatch with -insecure automatically.
  4. Press Load and follow the loader's status.

If you start Deathmatch yourself, add -insecure to its Steam launch options before loading Scooby.
