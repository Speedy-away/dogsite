Configure each target separately. Players and Unknown NPCs can use their own category settings or inherit Global; Self stays separate.

  • Rounding, outline, thickness and shadow
  • Corner length, pulse and screen offsets
Health bars and HP text

Armor bars
Free

Position and style armor bars separately from health.

Player and Self names

Distance labels

Adjust label position, size, colors, backgrounds and offsets.

Skeletons

Weapon names and icons
Free

Display the equipped weapon with adjustable icon size and position. Missing images fall back to a weapon label.

Cone hats

Rings

Ground rings with radius, height, count, segments, fill and pulse settings.

Trails

Offscreen indicators

Lock-on aimbot
Free

Camera aiming with a configurable aim key, FOV radius, distance, target priority, smoothing and movement prediction.

Lock-on hitbox selection
Free

Premium combat

2
Link to this section ↗
Silent Aim
Premium

Redirects an actual local weapon shot. Includes target bone, FOV, distance, priority, sticky target, prediction and ADS settings. Requires the matching shot and aiming bindings.

Magic Bullet
Premium

Actual-shot redirection with manual firing or Auto Fire. Shares Silent Aim target bone, priority, prediction, FOV, distance, ADS and damage settings, with target marker and line visuals. Requires compatible shot and attack-input bindings.
