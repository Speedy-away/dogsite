Is this a subscription?

The paid plans listed here are lifetime licenses with a one-time payment. Review your selected edition on its product page before purchasing.

Which GTA 5 edition should I choose?

Premium is $15 for lifetime access. Premium Plus is $25 and includes BattlEye support. The $10 upgrade is for existing Premium owners. See the GTA 5 product page for the full comparison.

How do free keys work?

Choose “Get a free key” and follow the unlock steps. Browse the free products, including CS2, GMod, Left 4 Dead, S&box, Spoofer + Cleaner and Half-Life. Read the free-key guide for details and limitations.

Can I use another payment method?

Alongside card and cryptocurrency, support can help with PayPal F&F, Skrill, Paysafe, and Steam Gift Cards. You can also browse resellers.
