Menu settings · Half-Life 1

Customize visuals, materials, language, hotkeys and profiles.

Menu settings

Visuals

Choose supported entity categories, then adjust boxes, names, distance, skeletons, snaplines and radar. NPC labels describe the model; they do not report server-only health or faction information.

Materials

Open Visuals → Materials. Choose Tinted, Flat, Wireframe or Additive, then adjust visible and occluded colors. Animated chams offer Pulse, Color fade and Rainbow.

Profiles, language and Lua

Save your settings in a profile and assign hotkeys to controls you use often. The shared interface includes language packs with English fallback. Product-specific strings may remain in English. Use the bundled Half-Life Lua examples for supported local UI and session APIs.
