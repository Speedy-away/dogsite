Getting started

Set up the free TF2 version, choose your edition and open the menu.

Getting started

  1. Download Scooby Launcher and sign in with a valid free key or your existing paid account. The same free-key session includes both TF2 editions; no separate TF2 purchase is needed.
  2. Open TF2 from Home or Source Games. Choose Team Fortress 2 for retail, or TF2 Classified for Classified, then select Continue.
  3. Enable Start game and press Load. The loader starts the selected Steam game with -insecure and waits for it to become ready. If the game is already open, it must have been started with that launch option.
  4. Start a local practice/listen-server map. The current native features and Lua game queries require an eligible local session.
  5. Press Insert or F1 to open the menu. Start with a few settings and save a profile once you are happy with them.

Asset downloads and languages

The launcher downloads the selected edition automatically. After loading, fonts and menu assets download automatically. A bar at the bottom of the game shows the filename and progress. Press F5 to retry a failed download; later starts reuse verified cached files. English is included. Download other languages from Settings > Interface > Download / update languages in-game.

For a missing menu or a game that stays on “Waiting for game,” follow troubleshooting.

1. Sign in — choose your access type

Use one of these two login methods.

Premium — use your Scooby account
  1. Choose Premium.
  2. Enter the username and password of the Scooby account you created.
  3. Choose Sign in. A purchased license key is redeemed on the portal; it is not your loader password.

New purchase? Redeem your Premium license key first →

Free — paste a free key
  1. Complete the free-key access steps and copy your key.
  2. In the loader, choose Free and paste it into Free key.
  3. Choose Sign in.

Free keys work only in the loader’s Free login. They cannot sign in to the portal/dashboard or be redeemed there as a Premium license.

One free key supports all Scooby Free versions. Free keys reset every 24 hours, so you need to get a new free key every 24 hours. Complete the access steps again, copy the new key and use it in the loader’s Free tab. You do not need a separate key for each game.

2. Choose your game

Open Games, then select TF2 & TF2 Classified.

Check that the dashboard name matches your game before continuing.

3. Check the edition, then Load

Follow the game-specific preparation steps above, check Edition if shown, and press Load once.

4. Wait for loading to finish

Keep the loader open while it prepares files and loads the module. A download percentage or “Preparing assets” message is still in progress; wait for the completion message.

Show the loading screen

5. Check completion, then return to the game

Look for Loaded and read the message beneath it.

If the menu does not open, check your configured menu key and the troubleshooting section for your game. Keep the exact error or status message.
