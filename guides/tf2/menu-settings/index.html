Menu settings and profiles

Adjust the interface, visuals, keybinds and edition-specific settings.

Menu settings and profiles

Use Insert or F1 to open or close the menu. The interface starts with main tabs at the top and subtabs at the bottom; later layout changes are saved.

  • Combat: separate Rage and Legit profiles, with their own activation controls. Set an aim or trigger key and choose Hold, Toggle or Always.
  • ESP: configure Enemy and Friendly players independently. Use the gear beside the player preview for class and team appearance. Item, building and world controls depend on the edition.
  • Chams: choose a target and configure Visible, Invisible and Overlay layers separately, including each layer’s color.
  • View and Movement: camera/model FOV and movement controls live under Misc. Additional controls differ between editions.
  • Settings: adjust menu scale, language and appearance, then save a named configuration under Configs.
  • Lua: run a bundled example or edit a script. See the Lua guide.

Retail skins and local inventory

Retail TF2 provides Misc > Skins for class appearances, loadout previews and cosmetics, plus Misc > Inventory for distinct local item copies. Items do not become Steam-owned or tradable. Native backpack changes are limited to supported main-menu and local listen-server sessions. Classified has no skins or inventory page.

Camera, movement and filters

Both editions include third person, free camera, viewmodel offsets, Heavy rev-jump and auto-strafe pre-speed. VFX filters control fog and individual screen effects. Settings > Capture Protection disables and locks protected visual and scene features.

Download a language

Open Settings > Interface and press Download / update languages. When the download finishes, choose your language from the Language list. This works in both menu layouts. English is included; required fonts arrive with the initial asset download, and other languages download in-game.

Language files are stored in your edition’s languages folder. Downloads preserve files you have edited. Use Reload languages after making a local change.

Keep your profiles

Native user data is separate from downloaded packages:

  • Retail: C:\scooby\TF2\TF2-Base
  • Classified: C:\scooby\TF2\TF2-classfied

Profiles, including default.json, are in configs; UI preferences are in configs/ui. Downloaded assets are in assets. Existing settings migrate automatically and conflicting copies are preserved. Use the menu’s folder controls to locate editable scripts and profiles. Back up your edition’s user-data folder before replacing a setup. The loader’s Open folder action opens its package/cache location, which is separate from your in-game profile data.

Stopping

The product’s Stop action disables its active hooks and restores owned input/render state. Restart the game before loading another build. Stopping an individual Lua script removes its callbacks and script UI; changes it made to built-in settings remain.
