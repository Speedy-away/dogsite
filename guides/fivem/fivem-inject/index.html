How to load Scooby in FiveM

Sign in, choose FiveM, and load at the right point in the game.

Load Scooby in FiveM

Loading stepsLoader pictureAlready in a server?Troubleshooting

Load in this order

  1. Complete the requirements, open the current Scooby loader, and sign in with your account or valid free key.
  2. Open Games and select FiveM.
  3. Choose Legacy in Edition for this supported loader path. If the loader says Enhanced loading is not configured, that edition cannot load through this path yet.
  4. Launch FiveM and wait at its main menu, or join a server and wait while its resources / assets are loading.
  5. Return to the loader and choose Load. Older loader versions may label this Inject. Press it once, then wait for the displayed preparation and loading steps to finish.
  6. Return to the game after the loader reports completion. Open Scooby using your configured menu key.

Already in a server?

Loading in-game is supported, but the main menu or resource-loading screen is the recommended point. If an attempt fails, close both apps and retry once from that point.

If Load is unavailable or fails

Check the selected game / edition, game-process status, access and the exact loader message. Follow antivirus & download help for a blocked file, or loader troubleshooting for a loading error. A paused or unavailable game needs its status resolved before loading.

1. Sign in — choose your access type

Use one of these two login methods.

Premium — use your Scooby account
  1. Choose Premium.
  2. Enter the username and password of the Scooby account you created.
  3. Choose Sign in. A purchased license key is redeemed on the portal; it is not your loader password.

New purchase? Redeem your Premium license key first →

Free — paste a free key
  1. Complete the free-key access steps and copy your key.
  2. In the loader, choose Free and paste it into Free key.
  3. Choose Sign in.

Free keys work only in the loader’s Free login. They cannot sign in to the portal/dashboard or be redeemed there as a Premium license.

One free key supports all Scooby Free versions. Free keys reset every 24 hours, so you need to get a new free key every 24 hours. Complete the access steps again, copy the new key and use it in the loader’s Free tab. You do not need a separate key for each game.

2. Choose your game

Open Games, then select FiveM.

Check that the dashboard name matches your game before continuing.

3. Check the edition, then Load

Follow the game-specific preparation steps above, check Edition if shown, and press Load once.

4. Wait for loading to finish

Keep the loader open while it prepares files and loads the module. A download percentage or “Preparing assets” message is still in progress; wait for the completion message.

Show the loading screen

5. Check completion, then return to the game

A success dialog confirms loading and offers Close loader.

If the menu does not open, check your configured menu key and the troubleshooting section for your game. Keep the exact error or status message.
