Red Dead Redemption 2 · Scooby Wiki

How to Launch Scooby

Follow these steps in order:

  • Sign into your Scooby account
  • Select RDR2
  • Launch Red Dead Redemption 2 and wait for it to load
  • Once you are at the main menu, click "▶️ LOAD" on the Scooby loader
  • You can enter story mode or online after the CMD console appears on screen

Menu doesn't appear in-game

You may need to change your graphics settings / resolution. Alternatively you may need to use a VPN.
