MTA:SA loading status

Open Games and select Multi Theft Auto: San Andreas. The current native loader shows Testing / Paused and does not offer a normal MTA loading action. Installing runtimes does not enable that button. Check the status in your current loader and wait for a supported release.

1. Sign in — choose your access type

Use one of these two login methods.

Premium — use your Scooby account
  1. Choose Premium.
  2. Enter the username and password of the Scooby account you created.
  3. Choose Sign in. A purchased license key is redeemed on the portal; it is not your loader password.

New purchase? Redeem your Premium license key first →

Free — paste a free key
  1. Complete the free-key access steps and copy your key.
  2. In the loader, choose Free and paste it into Free key.
  3. Choose Sign in.

Free keys work only in the loader’s Free login. They cannot sign in to the portal/dashboard or be redeemed there as a Premium license.

One free key supports all Scooby Free versions. Free keys reset every 24 hours, so you need to get a new free key every 24 hours. Complete the access steps again, copy the new key and use it in the loader’s Free tab. You do not need a separate key for each game.
