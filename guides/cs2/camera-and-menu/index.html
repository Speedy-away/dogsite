Camera, scopes & main menu

Customize rifle and sniper optics, fly a detached camera, or give the CS2 title screen its own scene and weather.

CS2 Camera, Scope Reticles and Main Menu Editor

Scope reticlesFreecamRear LookMain Menu Editor

Customize scope reticles

  1. Open Settings > Crosshair. Under Rifle Optics, enable Replace Rifle Dot for the AUG or SG 553. Under Sniper Scopes, enable Customize Sniper Scope for the AWP, SSG 08, SCAR-20 or G3SG1.
  2. Choose Hidden, Dot, Cross, Ring or Custom image. Use Import image to select a PNG or JPEG up to 512 × 512 and 4 MB. Check the result in Preview.
  3. Adjust the reticle size, color or image opacity. Sniper Scopes also has Lens opening and Outside opacity. Choose Restore defaults to reset the selected group.

Development builds add Full cross, Ring + dot and quick reticle presets. Freecam, Rear Look and the Main Menu Editor below also depend on your build. Check the feature list for availability.

Fly with Freecam

  1. With a living, unscoped player, press F7 or choose Visuals > Viewmodel > Freecam. Rebind F7 under Settings > Hotkeys.
  2. Use W, A, S and D to fly, Space and Ctrl to change height, and Shift for a speed boost. Close the menu to fly. Look with the mouse or arrow keys.
  3. Toggle Freecam off to return to the player. Freecam blocks player movement and firing while active. Flight pauses while the menu has focus.

Check the view behind you

Open Visuals > Viewmodel > Rear Look and assign a Hold key. Rear Look uses the picture-in-picture view. Scoping, third person, Freecam and native practice cameras take priority.

Edit the CS2 title screen

  1. At the title screen, open Custom > VFX > Main Menu and turn on Enable Main Menu.
  2. Use Scene & loadout to choose a stock scene and showcased items, or select a compatible compiled vanity map. To use a PNG, JPEG or GIF backdrop, choose Browse image or place the image in the folder opened by Open image folder. Select the image and turn on Replace 3D scene with image.
  3. In Panel layout, select a navigation button, card or other supported panel and adjust its position, scale, opacity or colors. Use Reset panel to restore that panel.
  4. Under Atmosphere, choose rain, snow, embers, dust or thunderstorm. Adjust haze and vignette separately. Thunderstorm also has its own lightning interval and thunder volume.

Use Restore stock menu to clear the title-screen changes and turn off its profile.
