Visuals & practice

Style Chams and map effects, set up the HUD, and find grenade lineups on the map overview.

CS2 Chams, World Effects, HUD and Grenade Practice

ChamsWorld effectsHUD themesEvent LogsGrenade practice

Choose a Chams material

  1. Open Visuals > Chams. Select a target and choose its Visible, Invisible or Overlay layer where offered.
  2. Choose Browse materials > Patterns for styles including Lightning, Galaxy and Plasma Coil. Adjust supported colors, opacity and motion in the material settings.
  3. For Visible Wireframe, use Show over model to draw the original model beneath the edges.

Development builds add Latex Fade, Pearlescent, Opal and Liquid Chrome under Finishes. Their settings depend on the selected material. Use Copy appearance, Paste appearance, Reset material or Undo to work on the selected layer.

The sections below describe development build controls. Check the feature list for availability.

Style the scene and hit effects

Open Visuals > World > Environment and choose a Global or map profile. Use Screen to choose a scene shader and adjust saturation and contrast. Map Effect adds edges, ripple, heat haze or pixelation. Use Sky & fog for cloud styling and Map Particles for ambient particle tint.

Under Visuals > World > Hit effects, choose a World Hit Effect or World Kill Effect, then set the style, color and opacity. Builds with on-demand effect assets prepare them when you select or enable a style.

Set up a HUD theme

Open Custom > HUD. Choose Minecraft, CS:GO, Counter-Strike: Source, Counter-Strike 1.6 or Half-Life and enable Enable custom HUD. Use the sample preview to adjust scale, opacity and individual elements. Hide replaced stock panels hides the panels covered by the enabled replacements.

Choose which events to log

Open Visuals > Overlays > HUD Panels > Event Logs. Choose the events you want and enable the overlay, Developer Console output or both. Use Preview Logs to check the layout. Drag the panel with the menu open and adjust background opacity or the number of visible entries.

Browse lineups and preview a landing point

  1. Open Misc > Helper > Library. Pick a map and floor, then select a grenade marker to see the lineup and available actions. Choose Expand overview for a larger map.
  2. Use Save library to save lineup edits. Save map image exports the selected overview as a PNG.
  3. Under Visuals > ESP > Grenades, turn on Grenade landing camera to preview the predicted endpoint while holding a grenade. Adjust distance and height in its settings.
