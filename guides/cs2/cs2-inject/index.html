Counter-Strike 2 · Scooby Wiki

How to Inject

There are two ways to get Scooby into CS2. Pick whichever suits where you are.

Load button (easiest)

Pressing Load will close CS2, reopen it and inject automatically. You do not need to do anything else - just let it restart the game.

Manual inject

If CS2 is already loaded and you do not want it restarted, use manual inject instead. You must be sitting on the main menu when you do it.

  • Have CS2 open and sitting on the main menu - not in a match
  • Choose manual inject in the loader
  • Wait for Scooby to load, then open the menu in game

Injection failing?

Make sure your antivirus is fully disabled and the loader is excluded from Windows Defender. If it still will not inject, work through Universal Troubleshooting in the General tab.
