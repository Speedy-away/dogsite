Getting started

The Last of Us Part I · Free and paid accounts

Getting started

  1. Download the Scooby Loader and sign in with your existing free-key or paid account.
  2. Select The Last of Us in the game library, then choose Part I in the game dropdown.
  3. Choose Start Game. In the popup, select The Last of Us Part I to launch through Steam. Part II is disabled as coming soon.
  4. Load a save. The dashboard detects the tlou-i.exe process and shows Part I: Running.
  5. Select Load. The loader downloads the native DLL and checks it against the module manifest before loading it.
  6. Press F1 or Insert to open the menu. Configure features and save a profile.

If you started Part I yourself, the dashboard detects it automatically; you do not need to launch it again.
