Getting started

Scooby S&box (Sandbox) is included with authenticated free-key and paid accounts. No additional S&box (Sandbox) key is required.

  1. Download the Scooby Loader and sign in with your existing free key or paid account. New to free keys? Read Getting a Free Key.
  2. Close S&box (Sandbox), select its card in the loader, then choose Launch S&Box.
  3. Choose Locate game if the loader cannot find your Steam installation. The loader downloads and verifies the matching runtime files.
  4. Press F1 or Insert to open the menu.
