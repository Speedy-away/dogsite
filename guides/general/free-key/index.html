Getting a Free Key

Follow these steps to get your Scooby free key:

  • Click the official link: https://bstlar.com/F/ScoobyKEY
  • Complete the steps on the page and follow the instructions. Can't complete the steps? Switch to Microsoft Edge or another browser
  • Once done, your key will appear on the page - copy the key shown
  • Open the loader, press "Free", then paste your key
The free key auto-resets every 4 hours - you must redo the steps to get a new one

Important

  • Only use the official link above to avoid scams
  • Close any ad pop-ups. The key will always show on the page itself - it is never an .exe
  • Do not share your key with anyone
  • After you get a key, download the loader from scoobymenu.cc - do not download from bstlar

Free Version Limitations

Free version is invite-only / solo

The free version is limited to invite only sessions or sessions with other modders in GTA Online. Joining a public lobby on the free version will crash.

If you have used Scooby before, delete the C:\scooby folder - the folder, not the loader. If you don't, it will probably crash due to old settings.
