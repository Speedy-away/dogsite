Get your free key

  1. Open the official Scooby key page.
  2. Complete the steps on that page. Your key appears on the page itself; copy the text.
  3. Download the loader from scoobymenu.cc. Open it, select Free, and paste your key.
  4. Open your game’s guide and follow its loading steps. Timing and access rules differ by game.

Renew your key every 24 hours

The free key resets every 24 hours. Return to the official key page and complete the steps again when you need a new key.

Having trouble on the key page?

  • Try an up-to-date Edge, Chrome or Firefox browser if the page does not complete.
  • Close advert pop-ups. The key is text, never an executable or a separate installer.
  • Get the loader from Scooby’s main website, not from an advert on the key page.
  • Paste the full key without extra spaces. If it has expired, generate a new one.
  • Keep your key private. Support does not need your password or key to read an error message.

Check the free version’s limits

For GTA Online, the free version supports invite-only / solo play or compatible sessions with other modders. It does not support normal public lobbies; joining one can crash the game.

Read GTA 5 Free Version Setup for the current game-edition and online-access instructions. If old settings cause a crash after an update, follow the settings-backup steps instead of deleting your files immediately.
