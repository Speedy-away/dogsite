Reset your HWID

Reset your hardware ID through the portal. Each reset has a 7-day cooldown.

Resetting Your HWID

7-day cooldown after every reset

After resetting your HWID, you must wait 7 full days before you can reset it again.

Do not open a support ticket to request a reset. Support will not reset your HWID for you or shorten the cooldown. If you are on cooldown, wait until it ends, then use the portal.

If you have changed hardware or are getting an HWID mismatch, follow these steps once your reset is available:

  1. Go to scoobymenu.cc/portal and log in
  2. Open the dropdown menu in the top right
  3. Click "My Profile"
  4. Scroll down to Hardware ID
  5. Click "Reset HWID"

Reset button unavailable? Wait until the full 7-day cooldown has ended, then try the portal again. Creating a ticket will not shorten the wait.
