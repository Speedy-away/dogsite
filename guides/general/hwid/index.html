Guides/General help/Reset your HWID

Reset your HWID

Reset your hardware ID through the portal. Each reset has a 7-day cooldown.

Resetting Your HWID

7-day cooldown after every reset

After resetting your HWID, you must wait 7 full days before you can reset it again.

Do not open a support ticket to request a reset. Support will not reset your HWID for you or shorten the cooldown. If you are on cooldown, wait until it ends, then use the portal.

If you have changed hardware or see an HWID mismatch, use the Reset HWID card on the portal dashboard. You can do this once your reset is available.

Reset from the dashboard

  1. Open scoobymenu.cc/portal and sign in to your account.
  2. Select Dashboard and find the Reset HWID card. On a phone, scroll down to it.
  3. Read Reset availability. If it says Ready to reset, you can continue. If it says Available in…, wait for the remaining time to end.
  4. Click Reset HWID. Read the confirmation, then select Reset HWID again when you are ready. Select Cancel if you do not want to reset.

These screenshots show the current portal with an example account. Your account name, products and remaining wait time will differ.

Confirm the reset

The confirmation opens before the reset is submitted. Check that you want to reset now: completing it starts the 7-day cooldown for your next reset.

If the button is unavailable

Available in… means your reset is on cooldown. The button stays disabled while you wait. After the full 7 days have passed since your last reset, refresh the dashboard to check availability again.

Support will not reset your HWID for you or bypass the cooldown. Do not create a ticket to request an early reset.

Can I still use My profile?

Yes. Open My account in the top right → My profile → Hardware ID. The same reset availability and 7-day cooldown apply. The dashboard is the shorter route.
