Premium and Free Setup

Sign in with Premium or a free key, choose your GTA 5 edition, and follow the loading steps.

GTA 5 Premium and Free Setup

Using Plus with BattlEye enabled?

Follow the Plus Setup Guide for its bootloader and reboot steps. This page covers Premium and Free with BattlEye disabled.

Before you start

  1. Install GTA 5 Legacy or Enhanced and confirm which edition you will launch.
  2. Download the current Scooby loader from the Scooby portal. Sign in to the website with your normal account if asked. A free key only works inside the loader.
  3. Install the required Visual C++ runtimes if needed, then restart Windows. Use the Microsoft x64 package and Microsoft x86 package.

Pictures below show the real native loader with example access and status values. Click a picture to enlarge it; use Zoom in for small labels.

1. Sign in — Premium or Free

Choose the login method that matches your access.

Premium — use the account you created
  1. Choose Premium in the loader.
  2. Enter your Scooby account’s username and password.
  3. Choose Sign in. Your GTA 5 Premium license must be activated on that same account.
Purchased a license key? Redeem it first
  1. Sign in to the portal with the account you will use in the loader.
  2. Open Dashboard → Redeem license key.
  3. Paste your purchased license key, choose Redeem key, and wait for the success message. Check that GTA 5 Premium appears on your account.
  4. Return to the loader and sign in using Premium with that account’s username and password. Sign out and back in if access needs refreshing.

A purchased license key activates your account; it is not your loader password. More account and activation help →

Free — paste a current free key
  1. Get a free key, complete the access steps and choose Copy Scooby key.
  2. In the loader, choose Free.
  3. Paste the copied key into Free key and choose Sign in.

One free key supports all Scooby Free versions. Free keys reset every 24 hours; get a new key every 24 hours and repeat the Free login. Free keys do not sign in to the portal/dashboard and cannot be redeemed as Premium licenses.

2. Choose GTA 5 and match your edition

  1. Open Games in the loader and select GTA 5.
  2. On the GTA 5 dashboard, set Edition to Legacy or Enhanced, matching the game you will start.
  3. Read your access and availability status. Resolve a Locked, Paused or unsupported-build message before loading.

3. Choose BattlEye Disabled

For the Premium and Free path on this page, choose BattlEye Disabled in the loader’s mode picker and select Continue. Launch GTA 5 with BattlEye disabled as required for that mode. Use the loader’s built-in BattlEye guide or the Legacy & Enhanced setup instructions for your launcher.

If you want the BattlEye Enabled option, use your eligible Plus access and complete the Plus Setup Guide before loading.

4. Load Scooby and open the menu

  1. Start the matching GTA 5 Legacy or Enhanced edition and wait at its main menu / landing page.
  2. Return to the Scooby GTA 5 dashboard and choose Load once.
  3. Keep the loader open while it prepares files and loads the module. Wait for the completion message; a download percentage alone is not completion.
  4. Return to GTA 5 and enter Story Mode.
  5. Open Scooby using the key for your interface below. If the menu does not appear, keep the exact loader message and follow the troubleshooting links.

5. Follow the online rules for your access

Free

Use solo / invite-only sessions with other Scooby users, with BattlEye disabled. Free does not support normal public lobbies or sessions with non-modders. Read the Free online-access rules before joining a session.

Premium

Premium uses BattlEye disabled for this loading path. Public-session hosting and playing with friends require you to remain the session host. Follow the Premium public-session hosting guide for the session requirements.

Plus / Premium Plus

The BattlEye-enabled path uses separate access and setup requirements. Continue with the Plus Setup Guide.

If something goes wrong

  • Website or loader cannot connect: use connection help. A backend or hosting outage may require waiting for service to recover.
  • Free key expired or rejected: get a current key and check that you chose Free.
  • Premium still locked: check that you redeemed the license on the same account used for Premium login.
  • File blocked or removed: follow antivirus & download help.
  • Crash, edition mismatch or no menu: use GTA 5 troubleshooting and keep the exact error text.
