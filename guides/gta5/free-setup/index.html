Free Version Setup (Legacy & Enhanced)

Using Plus with BattlEye enabled?

Follow the Plus Setup Guide for the reboot-based kernel loader and interface-specific menu keys.

Download VC Runtimes

Install the Visual C++ runtimes before launching Scooby. Choose the all-in-one package or install both Microsoft packages below, then restart your PC.

Need a key first? See Getting a Free Key in the General tab.

The free version is limited to solo play or invite-only sessions with other Scooby users. For public lobbies and playing with friends, see the Premium and Premium Plus requirements below.

If old settings cause a crash, rename the Scooby data folder as a backup and test fresh settings. Keep your scripts, outfits and presets in that backup.

For blocked files, use antivirus & download help. For connection errors, use the connection checklist. The free version runs with BattlEye off.

  • Check that the loader and its files have not been blocked or quarantined
  • Launch GTA 5
  • Inject Scooby when you reach the main menu / landing page
  • Wait for Scooby to load
  • Enter STORY MODE
  • Use the in-game pause menu to join an INVITE ONLY SESSION

GTA 5 Enhanced

  • Launch GTA 5 Enhanced
  • Inject Scooby when you reach the main menu / landing page
  • Wait for Scooby to load
  • Enter STORY MODE
  • Use the in-game pause menu or the Scooby menu to join an INVITE ONLY SESSION

Free, Premium, and Premium Plus: online play

Free — solo or other Scooby users only

With BattlEye disabled, you can play solo or in an invite-only session with other Scooby users. You cannot join public lobbies or play with non-modders on the free version. Joining a public lobby on the free version will crash.

Premium — host public lobbies and play with friends

Premium lets you host public lobbies and play with friends with BattlEye disabled. You must remain the session host at all times to avoid being desynced after about 3–5 minutes as a non-host.

Follow the Premium public-session hosting guide →

Premium Plus — play as host or non-host

Premium Plus supports BattlEye enabled, so you can join public lobbies and play with friends as either the host or a non-host. You do not need to stay the session host; Premium's host requirement does not apply.

1. Sign in — choose your access type

Use one of these two login methods.

Premium — use your Scooby account
  1. Choose Premium.
  2. Enter the username and password of the Scooby account you created.
  3. Choose Sign in. A purchased license key is redeemed on the portal; it is not your loader password.

New purchase? Redeem your Premium license key first →

Free — paste a free key
  1. Complete the free-key access steps and copy your key.
  2. In the loader, choose Free and paste it into Free key.
  3. Choose Sign in.

Free keys work only in the loader’s Free login. They cannot sign in to the portal/dashboard or be redeemed there as a Premium license.

One free key supports all Scooby Free versions. Free keys reset every 24 hours, so you need to get a new free key every 24 hours. Complete the access steps again, copy the new key and use it in the loader’s Free tab. You do not need a separate key for each game.

2. Choose your game

Open Games, then select GTA 5.

Check that the dashboard name matches your game before continuing.

3. Check the edition, then Load

Follow the game-specific preparation steps above, check Edition if shown, and press Load once.

4. Wait for loading to finish

Keep the loader open while it prepares files and loads the module. A download percentage or “Preparing assets” message is still in progress; wait for the completion message.
