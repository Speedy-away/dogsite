Playing onlineBattlEye Enabled (Plus) and BattlEye Disabled hosting

BattlEye Enabled (Scooby Plus)

With Scooby Plus running and BattlEye enabled, enter GTA Online as you normally would.

Stuck on the loading screen? If you enabled several of Scooby’s in-game event protections, turn most of them off and try joining again. These filters can block disruptive events from other players, but they can also block events GTA Online needs to load or play normally. Keep only the filters you need. Similar event filters can cause the same issue in other menus.
